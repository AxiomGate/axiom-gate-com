About

Enterprise habits, small-business scale

Axiom Gate brings the practices big companies pay for — security-first design, testing before launch, written documentation — to organizations that don't have an IT department.

Why we exist

Small businesses and nonprofits get squeezed from both sides. The tools they can afford lock them into monthly fees and hide the settings that matter. The consultants who understand those settings usually charge enterprise rates.

We work in between. We start by looking at what you already have, fix what's actually costing you, and build only what you need. When we're done, you get the keys: your domain, your code, your accounts, and plain-English notes on how it all fits together.

What we believe

  • Prove it works before calling it done. Everything we ship is tested, and we show you the evidence.
  • You own everything. No hostage domains, no proprietary platforms, no surprise renewals.
  • Your call, your business. When a decision is yours to make, we explain the trade-offs. We don't decide quietly for you.
  • Security isn't an add-on. It's in the name. Every site and tool we build starts from secure defaults.

Who we work with

Owner-run businesses, venues, clinics, and community nonprofits — teams small enough that "the IT person" is whoever is least busy that day.
