SMS Consent Documentation

How callers opt in to receive a text

The Axiom Gate business phone line, operated by AxiomGate Security Group LLC

Opt-in type: Verbal Channel: Inbound phone call Category: Customer care

How consent is collected

Axiom Gate’s business line is answered by an automated receptionist. Every number that receives a text from us got there the same way: the person dialed us first, and then said yes when asked.

The receptionist asks permission once, out loud, before any message is sent. If the caller declines, no text is sent and the call simply continues by voice. There is no web form, no purchased list, and no way to be messaged by us without having called and agreed on that call.

Call script excerpt

Caller

“I’d like to set up that thirty-minute call.”

Receptionist

“Happy to. Would Tuesday or Wednesday afternoon suit you better?”

Caller

“Tuesday around two.”

Receptionist

“That’s Tuesday, September 29th at two o’clock. Can I text a quick confirmation to the number you’re calling from?”

Caller

“Yes, that’s fine.”

System

Only at this point — after an explicit spoken yes — is the message below sent. A “no” ends the matter; the receptionist does not ask a second time.

Sample message sent after consent

Thanks for calling Axiom Gate! We have your free 30-minute call down for Tue Sep 29 at 2:00pm, and someone will follow up to confirm. Reply STOP to opt out.

The wording varies with what the caller asked for — it always confirms their request and that a person will follow up, and it always ends with the opt-out line. It is never marketing.

Program details

Message frequency
Occasional — a single confirmation per call, only when the caller agreed to it. Nothing recurring, no marketing, no promotional sends.
Rates
Message and data rates may apply.
Opt out
Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts.
Help
Reply HELP, or reach us any time at axiom-gate.com/contact.
Data use
Phone numbers collected on these calls are used only to send the confirmation the caller asked for and to follow up about their request. They are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing.
