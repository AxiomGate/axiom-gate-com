Services

Practical technology for teams without an IT department

Every engagement has a fixed scope agreed up front, a written handoff at the end, and no long-term contract.

01

Websites

Fast, secure, mobile-first sites built on plain, portable code, not a builder you rent forever. Typical sites load in under a second and cost little or nothing a month to host.

  • New builds and rebuilds of aging Squarespace, Wix, or WordPress sites
  • Inquiry and booking forms that land in your inbox
  • Content migrated carefully, including old photos, PDFs, and pages people still link to
  • Cost review of the booking and payment tools behind your site
  • You own the domain, the code, and every account
02

Findability

Google, Maps & AI search

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot for a recommendation before they ever search Google. We check what those assistants actually say about you, and why.

  • Google Business Profile review: name, category, hours, and policy risks
  • Site crawl for missing headings, duplicate pages, broken links, and thin content
  • AI-crawler access. Many site builders now block AI assistants by default.
  • Structured data (schema.org) and an llms.txt file so assistants read you correctly
  • A prioritized findings report in plain English, then the fixes if you want them
03

AI & automation

We build AI tools for specific, repetitive jobs, and measure whether they actually save time before calling them done.

  • AI phone receptionist that answers, takes messages, and texts you a summary
  • Inbox triage that sorts mail and flags what needs you
  • Content pipelines for scheduled posts, newsletters, and audio or video
  • Private, self-hosted options when your data shouldn't leave your control
04

Security checkups

A hands-on review of the basics that actually get small organizations breached, written for the owner, not an auditor.

  • Network and Wi-Fi assessment
  • Email, account, and password hygiene, including multi-factor sign-in coverage
  • Remote and home-office worker setups
  • A prioritized fix list, ranked by risk and effort
